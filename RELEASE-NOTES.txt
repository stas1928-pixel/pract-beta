Pract Beta 0.7.1-beta.1 — first externally distributable beta candidate

This build packages the tested Pract reader experience as a non-debuggable, release-signed Android beta. It uses a separate package (app.pract.reader.beta) and name (Pract Beta), so older development installs do not have to be removed.

Included:
- Full-screen continuous PDF reading, pinch zoom, and word/phrase selection.
- Simple contextual AI meanings and examples using your own eligible Gemini key.
- Saved vocabulary, bulk deletion, CSV export.
- Current-book resume, removable library cards, reading statistics, medium coral daily-goal semicircle.
- Expanded AI privacy, adult/region eligibility, and free-billing confirmation.
- Original copyright and bundled third-party notices.

Known limitations:
- No scanned-page OCR, EPUB, cloud backup, export import, or automatic transfer from the development app.
- Daily reading goal is still 30 minutes.
- Earlier development versions were exercised on the developer's Samsung device; this release candidate has desktop-browser and native unit checks, but still needs its first physical-phone installation check. Broader device compatibility is unverified.
- Browser/Android sideload warnings may appear. Never disable Play Protect to force installation.
- Own-key setup remains an early-beta compromise. Shared developer-funded AI and word-review/community features are not implemented.
